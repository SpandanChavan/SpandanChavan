### About Me

Final-year Computer Science student at **MIT-ADT University, Pune**. I like building AI products that people can actually use, and making sure they can be trusted.

- Building **LLM tools** with careful prompt design, where the answer is checked instead of blindly trusted
- Working on **deep learning for medical imaging**: brain tumour segmentation that runs on ordinary hardware
- Shipping **full-stack apps** with real-time data, maps and dashboards
- Completed a **Generative AI internship** at Prodigy InfoTech
